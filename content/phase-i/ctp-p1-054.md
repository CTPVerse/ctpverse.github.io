Many individuals have had Near Death Experience (NDE). It happens when there is so much trauma or damage to the physical or P-body that the C-body is about to “check out” however it is still connected via C-energy Cord to the physical body and the rest of C-energy Units existing behind the individual atoms and molecules of the physical body.

Once C-energy Cord is severed or disconnected the C-body can never return back to its original physical or P-body. That is the final physical death. During an NDE the person’s C-energy is highly focused on the C-body including with his or her Ego Mind thus memories of such events are much clearer when the person comes back than in a typical dream.

In an NDE case the person’s C-body always returns to the P-body and if the person has a clear memory of the experience then he or she refers to it as a Near Death Experience. A lot of individuals go through an NDE, but have no memories of it because their Ego Mind blocked most or all of them.
