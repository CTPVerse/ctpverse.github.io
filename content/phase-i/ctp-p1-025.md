P-Domain Objects Mover™ works on a simple principle already being done by some individuals today with their mind. Such individuals have a very high focus and intensity with their C-body and C-energy (hundreds of times greater than in an average person) so when they send out a thought to a target like a spoon to bend or float, the C-energy Units existing behind the spoon pick up this thought and execute the action of bending a spoon (on atomic level) or moving a physical object. Human C-Body has billions of C-energy Units behind it and thus has a lot more power in manipulating the physical world and can effect lesser number of C-energy Units present behind an object such as spoon. A CTP Energy System called P-Domain Objects Mover™ will employ such a C-energy amplification of a person’s thought directed at an object that needs to be moved and such action will take place – the target object will move.
