Just as C-energy creates new atoms of different physical worlds including ours all the time, it also creates many different species.

A species, as a group consciousness made of many individual entities, enters a physical world at a particular time. It exists as a group for a certain period of time and then it leaves such physical world never to return to the same physical world again. An entity of a particular species in C-domain is a specialized group of C-energy Units desiring to be such a specific species in a physical domain. The actual thought-desire to have a particular physical life existence drives the kind and the number of C-energy Units the C-Body of such species will have which in turn drives the shape of their physical bodies.
