The integration of your C-Body with your physical or P-Body begins at some point during the fetus development of the baby. From that moment on and especially after the physical birth the focus shifts from C-domain to dealing with the P-domain via new body and brain.

The brain, used for processing of physical-chemical signals for the P-body in the physical domain only , is completely unwired at the birth. This is the case on purpose. This creates totally separate and brand new memories and experiences of the new physical life.

We choose physical life because we want to experience the three dimensional physical world with time, distance, physical touch, taste, which are non-existent in the C-domain. It is again a choice. There are personalities in C-domain that never enter any physical domain and never have any physical life. Most do.
