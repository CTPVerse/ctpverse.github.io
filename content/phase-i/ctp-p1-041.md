When you go to sleep and dream, you can have two kinds of dream experiences. One where your C-Body or spiritual body does not leave your physical or P-Body and you are dreaming. You are still experiencing the reality via your C-body, but it has not actually left your physical body yet.

Another kind of experience you can have during your dream is when your C-body actually leaves your physical body. It always does that during the REM (Rapid Eye Movement) part of your sleep. REM is always a sign that the C-Body of a person sleeping has left the P-Body. It is still connected to the physical body and C-energy Units behind your body molecules and cells via the C-energy Cord.

Your ego mind will always translate all of your dreams or C-domain experiences in the terms and symbols of physical life. To have actual memories of your direct C-domain trips and experiences, one has to turn off the ego mind translator. Still to you, experiences you have while being in different C-domain locations (as related to C-energy frequency) will seem like a mumbo jumbo sequence of events most of the time.
