Your Inner or Higher Self is much greater than your Ego Self (a by product of your physical life). You choose your physical existences from the C-domain. You have other Aspects of what we call your total Higher Self which exist in C-domain. You are connected via C-energy with other Aspects of your Higher Self in C-domain even when you exist in the physical or P-domain. Each Aspect has it own distinct physical existence in P-domain and memories of the same.

The same Aspect does not come back to experience different physical time periods or existences as some reincarnation theories suggest. Other Aspects of your Higher Self each have a physical time period existence to experience physical life and typically there is an evolutionary sequence in relation to what each Aspect is about and when it enters the P-domain. The Aspect that is in the physical world today receives “bleed through” messages and thoughts on a daily basis from other Aspects who have already been through physical experience or yet to have one.

Your current interests and feelings for other than what makes you the you in the physical world of today give you some indications of who your Aspects are or were. At the end of the sequence of Aspectual experiences in physical life or domains, all Aspects (C-bodies) unite into an even higher being and level of C-domain existence with memories and experiences of all physical lives and events of all Aspects of your Higher Self now being in one spiritual entity.
