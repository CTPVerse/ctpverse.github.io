There are individuals in the world who can sense conditions for a particular event that will happen. Remember an event’s timing in physical world is not a predictable quantity. Time is a by product of creation of conditions for an event to happen thus it is a flexible entity.

Conditions for an event to happen are actually a known entity in C-domain. All kinds of events have been sequenced in C-domain. We often go through such sequences while in C-domain during sleep. Sometimes they happen exactly as experienced in C-domain and we the feel them as Déjà vu.

If one has a premonition of something it does not mean such an event will actually happen (all of conditions for it to happen may not occur) and we only recognize them as such if they actually do happen or similar close-enough probable events occur. We all have the capacity of Premonition, but like everything else in life and in C-domain practice makes it better.
