Thoughts of groups of C-energy Units then get translated into the vibrations of a physical world or P-domain via another set of C-energy Units called Translational or T-energy Units. The spreading of each C-energy Unit in C-domain is not sp acial

(distance or volume), but it occurs only along different C-energy frequencies or vibrations.

Different C-energy frequencies mean different states of reality in the C-domain and not different sp acial locations since there is no physical space, distance and time in C-domain.
