Many individuals have had Out of Body Experiences (OBEs). Every person has memories of such but many of us attribute the same to just dreams. Our C-body leaves our physical or P-body every night and it is always out during the REM part of sleep.

Our C-body needs to spend time in C-domain just as our Physical Body has to spend time being active in P-domain and for the same reason – to re-energize itself. The moment your C-body leaves your P-body the Higher Mind takes over your thought and decision process. Ego Mind could not handle the multi-channel inputs C-body experiences in C-domain.

Ego Mind and our 99% focus on it is the reason why most of us have very poor memories of such C-domain experiences because we try to use Ego Mind for figuring out memories from dreams or out of body experiences and it is not directly involved in the processing of C-domain experiences . Ego Mind translates them into the symbols of physical life anyway.

Occasionally we have a very vivid memory of being out of our physical body and doing something unusual in C-domain and we then call such vivid dream experiences Out of Body Experiences (OBEs), but in reality we do this every single night.
