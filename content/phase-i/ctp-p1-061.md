Planet Earth is the largest conglomeration of C-energy Units near us – trillions upon trillions of them. Planet Earth has the capacity to react to imbalances of C-energy it is subjected to or it senses. It does not have the capacity to think in a focused and sophisticated way as we humans do with our C-bodies, but it has the power of the volume of C-energy Units to react with. It reacts in the only way it knows how to – earthquakes, hurricanes, tsunamis, tornados, hot weather, rains, floods. We humans are the source of most of this imbalanced C-energy our Planet Earth is reacting to.
