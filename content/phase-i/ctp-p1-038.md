When a person has what we call Clairvoyance, that person has the capacity to sense upcoming events or see things that are "far away" in the physical world or connect with a person that is "physically dead" or missing.

All of this comes from this person's capacity to pick up information via C-domain. Often, such a person can hold an object owned by a missing person such as a person's keys and see what happened to them or where they may be. Here is the CTP science behind this.

The C-energy Units that exist behind the atoms of a set of keys of a missing person have a certain capacity to store or remember memories or images of the events and memories of the original owner of the keys transferred to them via that person’s own C-Body and C-energy Units.
