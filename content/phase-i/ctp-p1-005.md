Just as an atom exhibits, in its smallest form, the characteristics of a particular matter in the physical world or P-domain, a C-energy unit represents, in its most basic form, the smallest unit of C-energy entity that can “think” in C-domain of being something in other realms (energies/vibrations), including our own physical universe.

These “thoughts” of one or more C-energy units are then translated into the vibrations of our and other physical worlds (and most are not visible from the frequency of our physical world) via another set of energy units that we call Translational or T-energy Units.

T-energy Units are “plugged into” the C-energy Units and translate their thought vibrations into the proper codes and frequencies which then exhibit themselves in different physical worlds including ours as atoms, gravity, electric or magnetic field and other physical forces and energies.

T-energy Units act like the “printer cables” of the C-world that with “proper software” connect our physical world to the C-domain and vice versa.
