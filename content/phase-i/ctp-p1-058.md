Physical life and how it relates to events is probably the least understood by all of us. We are so focused on the physical life that we do not even think about the fact that it only exists for each fraction of time or in the short Now.

All other probable and filtered events belong to the C-domain – those that have not happened yet and those that have happened and have been filtered-chosen by us.

There is no other Now. There is no other physical world – yet we choose to ignore so much of what we are a part of in a much, much greater way – the C-domain where most of our events and conditions for events are.

The point Now where the events coming from C-domain finally materialize at each and every instant in the physical world is called the P-Domain V-Point. Each little-short Now is all of the physical world we experience and nothing else.
