Some Crystals not only have unique crystal lattice structure allowing them to be used as semi-conductors in the electronics industry, but even more importantly, when used as whole crystal rods (rather than as cut wafers for chips) they have a unique ability to focus and amplify the C-energy. No other natural material has greater power and ability to that than certain crystals.

Different crystals have different capacity to focus different kinds of C-energy as related to different thought. Thought means action in C-domain and different types of crystals are better or worse for different kinds of thought amplifications.
