Meditation gives us a chance to sense so much greater self we have via our C-body and to change our focus from psychical world and Ego Mind to our inner self and mind. This in turn also gives us more power in the Physical World. Individuals who use their C-body and inner self on a daily basis do much better in life than those who do not.
