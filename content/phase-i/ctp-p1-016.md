This is one of the most important understandings of CTP Energy Science.

There are two kinds of entities relative to C-energy content they have: Single Layer C-energy Entities and Double Layer C-energy Entities. All matter or objects that we refer to in physical world as “dead matter” are Single Layer C-energy Entities. All entities we refer to in our physical world as

“live beings” are Double Layer C-energy Entities.

A Double Layer C-energy Entity has two layers (groups) of C-energy Units behind it. One layer of C-energy Units together with associated T-energy Units actually creates physical atoms and molecules of the physical body of such entity. The Second layer of C-energy Units is the spiritual or C-body of such entity made of its own separate C-energy U nits ( there are no associated T-energy Units for C-bodies of any kind) which is then integrated with the physical body of such an entity.

All live beings belong to Double Layer C-energy Entities including humans, animals, plants, single cell organisms. The “live” part comes from the fact that Second Layer of C-energy Units or their C-body can generate independent thoughts of action and not just thoughts as related to the creation of basic atoms and matter. The power of such C-energy Units is much greater than of C-energy Units existing behind the creation of atoms only.
