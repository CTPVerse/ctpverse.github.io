A T-energy Unit if a form of C-energy thus it behaves a lot like C-energy. It is very similar to C-energy Unit in energy structure, shape and behavior, but it is of a somewhat different color in C-domain. T-energy Unit is mostly invisible in the P-domain.

However, a T-energy Unit in its final function-purpose-form becomes visible in a particular physical world because it codes the vibrations of such physical world relative to C-energy Units behind it. Thus T-energy can also behave like P-energy.

Atoms in our physical world exist thanks to particular T-energy Units that are 'tuned' into the range of our physical world C-energy frequencies and connected to a particular set of C-energy Units in C-domain 'thinking' of being something or someone in our physical world.

Each range of physical frequencies of C-energy frequencies has its own set of T-energy Units. The same group of C-energy Units existing in C-domain can appear differently (based on different thought of the same group of C-energy Units being something in different physical worlds) in different P-domains via different T-energy Units. There exists a multiple set of T-energy Units for each particular C-energy group of Units in C-domain for the purpose of different P-domain coding and existences.

T-energy Units are are literally 'plugged into' the C-energy Units in C-domain and translate their thought vibrations into the proper codes and frequencies which then exhibit themselves in different physical worlds as different matter and entities. T-energy Units are the printer cables of the C-world that with 'proper software' connect our physical world to the C-domain and vice versa.
