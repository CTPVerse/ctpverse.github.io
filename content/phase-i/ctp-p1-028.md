C-Energy Frequency Modulator™ is an instrument that will allow near instant travel from our physical world or P-domain to any other range of C-energy frequencies or realities or physical worlds. In C-domain the range of C-energy frequencies defines a reality or a physical world. Also each group of C-energy Units have their own distinct C-energy frequency directly depended on the number and type of C-energy Units an entity is made of. Travel in C-body or via a physical spaceship using C-energy Frequency Modulator™ takes place nearly instantly if these C-Energy frequencies are known and made as a desired destination. There is no physical space travel – just change-shift in reality location via change of C-energy frequency range (defining universe you want to be at) and having specific target C-energy frequency (who you want to visit there) within that C-energy frequency range. A spaceship using C-Energy Frequency Modulator™ can simply just appear near its physical world destination (a planet, for example) and then use the CTP Gravity Control System™ to maneuver further within that physical world.
