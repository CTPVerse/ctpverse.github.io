From the second your C-body is integrated with your Physical or P-Body (fetus) in the physical world, you begin to form your Ego Mind. From that moment on and thanks to the creation of wirings of your brain and linear processing of events, you begin to create the memories of sequential events in the physical domain.

These memories of your physical life only are stored in your brain cells, the C-energy Units existing behind the creation of brain cells and in the C-energy Units of your total C-Body. We call these memories your Ego Mind. Again, the Ego Mind is made of memories of your physical life only.

Your Ego Mind will always refuse to accept events as they happen or as seen in C-domain via your C-body and Higher Mind. Your Ego Mind always wants to translate any such experiences in C-domain in terms of physical life only. Thus, most of the time, memories of your C-domain experiences will not be true representations of the same but mainly only symbolic ones.

Your Ego Mind translator must be turned off or your Ego Mind taught how to remember C-domain experiences as they truly happened in order to have memories of the same upon return to P-domain.
