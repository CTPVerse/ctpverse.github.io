This is one of the most important understandings of CTP Energy Science.

There are two kinds of entities relative to C-energy content they have: Single Layer C-energy Entities and Double Layer C-energy Entities. All matter or objects that we refer to in our physical world as “dead matter” are Single Layer C-energy Entities. All entities we refer to in our physical world as “live beings” are Double Layer C-energy Entities.

A Single Layer C-energy

Entity has just one layer (group) of C-energy Units in C-domain thinking of being something in a physical domain such as a rock in our physical world. We see this group of C-energy Units in our physical world as a rock thanks to the coding of their thoughts by particular type of T-energy Units into the exact vibrations of our specific physical world only (a particular narrow range of C-energy frequencies that defines our physical world) that we can then see.

All “ dead matter” as we see it in our physical world are Single Layer C-Energy Entities such as rocks, crystals, water, metal, plastic, other. Again such objects have only one layer of C-energy Units and corresponding T-energy Units creating their atoms in our physical world.

There is 1:1 correlation between atoms we see in the physical world and T-energy Units behind them. The correlation between atoms and C-energy Units of such Single Layer C-energy Entities is different because the number of C-energy Units behind an object is always smaller that atoms – usually 10:1 or even 100:1 in favor of atoms or T-energy Units relative to the number of C-energy Units existing behind an object.
