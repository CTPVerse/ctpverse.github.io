Being in C-Domain is a completely different experience than being in a physical world. Physical worlds are three dimensional with a sense of distance, time and space.

C-Domain is broken down into millions of ranges of C-energy Frequencies, each range defining a reality. Some are non-physical and some are physical realities. In non-physical realities, there is no sense of time, distance and space.

Learn more about how you can travel via C-domain in your C-Body – we all do it every night; no spaceships required.
