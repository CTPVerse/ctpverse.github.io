Have you ever wondered how animals can sense natural disasters like earthquakes or approaching tornados before they happen?

Well, the reason why they can do that is their C-Body or group of C-energy Units. If an earthquake happens anywhere on the Planet Earth, the information on this initiated disturbance within the conglomeration of C-energy Units called Planet Earth travels via C-domain instantly.

It takes several minutes for the earthquake shock wave to reach the surface of the Earth. C-energy Units or C-Bodies of most of the animals pick up these signals right away and thus react to an impending disaster in the physical domain because they "know" about it before it happens.
