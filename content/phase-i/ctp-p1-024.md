C-Domain Communicator™ is an instrument that will allow any person in the physical world or P-domain to communicate with any one anywhere in C-domain or to reach any frequency range in C-domain. C-Domain Communicator™ will operate in no different way than a cell phone except that the medium of sending the information-waves will be C-domain and C-energy and target recipient of particular C-energy frequency as defined by C-energy frequency range (your C-domain destination reality or world) and specific C-energy frequency of the target within the chosen C-energy frequency range. The instrument will be able to operate both ways communication wise with desired target (if it is capable of communicating back via C-domain).
