Your C-Body or Spiritual Body or Spirit or Soul is one of the most unique conglomerations or groups of C-energy Units existing in C-domain, certainly the most dense (C-energy Units wise) and one of the most capable of all existing in the C-Domain.

In C-Domain most of the times your C-Body has a spherical shape. That is its natural shape. We are used to human shape (an thus it often has such human shape in C-Domain) because of our physical body existence and ego-mind non-acceptance of any other shape. C-Body carries-stores for ever all of your memories of all of your life - both physical and non-physical ones occurring during sleep.

During one's physical life the C-Body is attached to the Physical Body or P-Body via C-energy Units existing behind all cells, molecules and atoms of one's Physical Body. It is attached to them via a C-energy connection on the back of one's neck. We call it C-energy Cord or some may call it spiritual cord and it resembles that. The connection is not direct with physical body cells or atoms. No mater how far one travels in C-Domain during the physical sleep (remember it is not a physical space travel), one's C-Body is always connected via C-energy Cord to the P-body left behind.

Should there be an emergency around or in connection with the physical body or there is a physical sound or some other physical interruption of the sleep, the C-Body is instantly recalled by the C-energy Units of the P-Body. The C-Body is the "software" that makes the P-Body work. Without it the P-Body is paralyzed. C-energy Units that exist behind physical cells are there for that reason and only to make sure various parts and organs of our physical body function properly all the time (including when C-Body is out of P-Body every night) and cannot and do not substitute the function of the C-Body. During physical REM sleep our C-Body is always out in the C-Domain.

When P-Body dies, the C-Body is permanently detached from it and it continues its pure non-physical C-domain existence that it never really left. During a 24-hr physical world day we spend around a third of that time in C-domain, and some of us spend less - one reason for importance of getting enough sleep - do not shortchange your C-Body. The C-Body must do that to regenerate itself every night and "stay alive" so to speak.
