If we are spiritual beings, which we are, then we should not fear physical death, but most of us do. That is so because our ego and ego mind (by product of our physical life) is, for most of us, our dominant mind. Ego mind is here to protect your physical body which, yes, can die and it knows once it does that it is gone forever!

However, your higher mind or C-body has been around forever in C-domain and it knows the permanency of one’s existence and looks at physical life as a physical experience that was planned for or chosen by itself. We choose how long we want to live in the physical world. Yes, unexpected events can happen to shorten it but most of the time, if someone’s physical life was short, then that was meant to be or is part of the plan of a short existence in the physical world.

Time is irrelevant in C-domain so only our ego mind fears time or loss of time that happens in an early physical death. From the C-domain prospective, the only things that matter are experiences, memories and events created in the physical domain. How long or short of a time it takes to do that does not matter at all in C-domain.
