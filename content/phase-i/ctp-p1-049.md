Your spiritual or C-Body is made of billions of C-energy units that serve as a memory bank for all of your memories of experiences in C-domain and those memories stored as part of your physical life.

Every single C-energy unit your C-Body is made of is involved in storing all of your memories – they are never lost. This mind is what we call the Higher Mind.

When you are in C-domain you use the mind of your whole C-Body or your Higher Mind to interact with your surroundings. Your Ego Mind would not be capable to handle the inputs and experiences as you truly have them in C-domain from many channels and realities all at once.

Your C-Mind or Higher Mind can do that. However Higher Mind also suppresses the use of Ego Mind when in C-domain . Also Ego Mind translates C-domain experiences in the terms of physical life experiences, so the relationship between the Ego and Higher Mind is a very complex one.
