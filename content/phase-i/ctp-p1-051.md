Probably the most difficult concept to accept about C-Domain and C-energy is the fact that any communication or thought via or in C-domain is instant in action - no time needed, no space to be traveled, no limit of speed of light for information to get somewhere.

Even harder to accept than Einstein's theories is the fact that "where" you are in C-domain has nothing to do with space, location and time. "Where" you are in C-domain has everything to do with the range of C-energy frequencies you find yourself in. Conscious or C-energy is infinite in its scope of existences however they are defined and separated by C-energy ranges. Other ranges of C-energy frequencies are not visible from another.

However it is all energy, very special non-physical energy and because it is non-physical energy, it behaves in a very different way. There again is no space, volume, distance or physical world in C-domain. Because of its non-physical nature, C-energy allows for instant communication and finds its desired destination immediately.
