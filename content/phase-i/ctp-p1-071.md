An aura is typically considered as an energy field of colored layers which carry within them the imprint of our emotional and physical state. Also some believe that one’s aura attracts you or repels you from them.

Aura is actually by product of reaction of our C-body and C-energy it emanates constantly (thoughts) and reacting with T-energy Units existing behind atoms and molecules of our physical body which are created by a different set of C-energy Units in charge of creating them.

The lock-connection between our spiritual or C-body and our physical body takes place between C-energy Units of our C-body and C-energy Units existing behind the creation of our body atoms and molecules. Thus C-energy of our C-body can react with T-energy behind the atoms and molecules of our physical body. This interaction creates the visible color or Aura and it does reflect our thoughts or state of mind which is all contained in our C-body.
