### Forming the core of CTP Energy as a company, from the ground up

**Overview.** CTP Corporate Formation is the corporate startup function within CTP. The function is responsible for forming the core of CTP Energy as the company, from the ground up, and for establishing the corporate foundation on which the wider CTPVerse is built.

**Scope.**

- Company formation — establishing CTP Energy as the company.
- Corporate structure — defining the structure supporting the CTPVerse.
- Operating foundation — building the core functions required to operate the company.
- Founding team — assembling the team responsible for building the company.

**Founding team.** CTP Corporate Formation is seeking a full founding team of venture builders — founders, operators, and corporate talent — to build the company itself. Founding professionals with prior experience in this work will help build the function within CTP from zero, from the ground up. The company is being built internally, as a founding-stage enterprise.

**Mission context.** CTP is a Corporate Enterprise unlike anything experienced previously. Its mission objectives include building the transition of human civilization from a terrestrial civilization to a post-terrestrial civilization, toward a multiplanetary, interplanetary civilization within a 15 to 20 year timeline.

**Participation profile.** Participation is intended for professionals who want to be part of building the future of civilization. This is not a horizontal career move. It is a founding-stage opportunity to participate in the formation of the company and in the transition described above.
