Financing Arm · CTP Capital Group

### The capital foundation for the CTPVerse

**Overview.** CTP Capital Group is the financing arm of the CTPVerse and the foundation for raising all funds for the CTPVerse. The Group provides the capital structure through which CTPVerse initiatives are financed and developed.

**Mandate.**

- Raise capital for the CTPVerse across all business units and lanes.
- Finance and support the build-out of CTPVerse business units and lanes.
- Serve as the central financing function for the CTPVerse.

**Founding team.** CTP Capital Group is seeking founding capital professionals with experience in capital services and the capital industry, and with a track record of having performed this work previously. Founding participants will assist in forming the Group, defining its responsibilities and duties, and building the function within CTP from zero, from the ground up.

**Formation approach.** Founding participants contribute their own resources and allocator relationships to create the structure. The Group is being built internally, from the ground up, as a founding-stage function within CTP.
